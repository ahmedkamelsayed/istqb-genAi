How the questions are phrased

  • Matching — match items 1–4 to descriptions A–D (you pick the mapping a/b/c/d).
  • “BEST explains / BEST describes / BEST suited” — several options are plausible; pick the strongest.
  • “Which is INCORRECT” — three options are true, one is false (the false one is the answer). Read carefully.
  • “Select TWO” — multi-answer; you must get both.
  • Scenario K3 — given a testing situation, choose the right technique / sequence / prompt. These are the 2-pointers.

Answer-technique tips

  1. K3 prompt questions — map the scenario to the right technique:
    • complex task, needs step-by-step checking → prompt chaining
    • repetitive / fixed output format, examples available → few-shot prompting
    • flexible/new task, or you don’t know how to word it → meta prompting
    • no examples, rely on model knowledge → zero-shot
  2. “INCORRECT” questions — flip your thinking: you’re hunting the false statement.
  3. On “BEST” questions, eliminate options that are true but off-topic — the answer must address the specific goal in the stem.
  4. Watch for distractors that swap definitions (e.g., calling chaining “providing examples”).
  5. Recurring vs. non-recurring cost, privacy vs. security, monitoring vs. control, hallucination vs. reasoning error vs. bias — these paired distinctions recur.

A 2-week plan (adjust to your timeline)

  • Days 1–2: Chapter 1 summary + flashcards (terms). Foundations make everything click.
  • Days 3–6: Chapter 2 — read twice; practice the 6-component prompt; drill the technique-selection table until automatic.
  • Days 7–8: Chapter 3 — memorize the paired definitions and mitigation lists.
  • Day 9: Chapters 4 & 5 — vocabulary recall (RAG, agents, fine-tuning, LLMOps, shadow AI, adoption phases, roles).
  • Day 10: Take Official ISTQB Exam A timed → review every wrong answer against the chapter summary.
  • Days 11–12: Re-study weak chapters; take Practice Exam B timed.
  • Day 13: Re-take both, aim ≥ 85 %. Re-read flashcards.
  • Day 14: Light review only. Rest.
